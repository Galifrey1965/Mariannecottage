We are Mark and Kim, hosts of a small farmhouse on a quiet lane in the Bessin. Marianne Cottage is two bedrooms, two centuries of stone, and breakfast at our kitchen table — five minutes from Cerisy-la-Forêt Abbey, half an hour from the D-Day beaches, and a long way from anything in a hurry.

The cottage

An 1800s longère, kept honest

The longère is the long, low farmhouse of rural Normandy — built from local stone, lime-washed walls, oak beams worked by hand. We have restored ours sympathetically: the original character preserved, the comforts modern, nothing pretending to be older or newer than it is.

Inside, two bedrooms (one double, one twin), a shared bathroom, a sitting room with a wood-burner, and a breakfast table that catches the morning light. Outside, an orchard of Normandy apple trees, a garden the roe deer wander through at dawn, and a sky that refuses to hurry.

A stay here is unhurried by design. Read on the terrace. Walk the lanes. Hear the church bell at six. Eat breakfast we have made for you. Then, if you wish, drive ten minutes and be standing in front of an abbey founded in 1032.

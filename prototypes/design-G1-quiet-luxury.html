The cottage

Old stone, kept honest.

This is a 1800s Normandy farmhouse — built from local Pierre de Caen, lime-washed, oak beams worked by hand. We have kept ours that way. Two bedrooms, one shared bathroom, a sitting room with a wood-burner, and a breakfast table that catches the morning light.

Outside: an orchard of Normandy apple trees, a garden the roe deer cross at dawn, and a sky that refuses to hurry. The church bell at the village rings the hours.

We have done this work over time, not to a deadline. It is finished now, and it is finished honestly. Nothing pretends to be older or newer than it is.
